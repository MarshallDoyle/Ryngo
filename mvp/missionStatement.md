# Ryngo — Mission

Most products today are too complex for the people who own them.

A PM ships features without a map of the system underneath. An exec
greenlights a refactor without seeing what it touches. A vibe coder
generates a codebase from prompts and within a week can't answer what
their own product actually does. Engineers fix this by reading the
code, which takes hours and doesn't scale to anyone else in the room.

The standard answer is "ask the LLM." It's a bad answer. LLMs
hallucinate structure that isn't there, miss structure that is, and
require a fresh deep-read on every question. The reply is a paragraph
of text — gone the moment you scroll. It is not a map. It is not
shared. It is not the same one your AI agent will see tomorrow.

Ryngo is one map of your codebase, drawn from the code itself, shared
by everyone — humans and AI agents alike. Typed, deterministic, loads
in seconds, updates on every commit.

For PMs and execs: a dashboard. See the product as a graph. See
what's new this week. See where complexity is concentrating. Share
the view with stakeholders who will never `git clone` anything.

For vibe coders: a feedback loop. Generate code, see it as a
structure, mark what should change, hand the marks back to your AI
of choice. The loop closes because Ryngo speaks the LLM's language
natively — every node has a stable id, every annotation lives in a
markdown file your agent can read, every change shows up as a visual
diff against your stated intent.

For engineers: a shared frame. Stop explaining the codebase. Send a
link.

We are not a coding agent. We are the map your coding agent is
missing. Deterministic, no hallucinations, no retraining, the same
view at ten files and at ten thousand.

— ryngo.ai
